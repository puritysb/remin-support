Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-13

Summary

  • Remin requests access to your device's Photos for photo/video analysis.
  • Analysis is designed to be performed on-device whenever possible.
  • Remin does not include advertising or tracking SDKs and does not sell data to third parties.

Information We Access

Remin may access the following data on your device to provide app functionality:

  • Photos/videos and metadata (capture time, location if available): For travel photo curation, search, and content creation features

Storage

Remin may store analysis results and settings locally on your device (e.g., local database, cache) for app operation.

Server Transmission

Remin does not upload photos or videos to our own servers.

However, if you use iCloud Photo Library, photos may be downloaded to your device through network connections managed by the iOS system (Apple services).

Tracking

Remin does not track users.

Permissions

  • Photo access permission: For photo selection, analysis, and album creation features
  • (Optional) Location permission: May be used when map/route display features are enabled

Data Deletion

When you delete the app, Remin's local data stored on your device is also removed. (Note: Original photos and albums are managed by you in the Photos app.)

Contact

For inquiries and support, please see our Support page.

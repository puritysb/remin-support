Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30

Summary

  • Remin requests access to your device's Photos to analyse your photos and videos.
  • All analysis — clustering, quality scoring, search indexing and AI-written travel stories — runs on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to us.
  • Remin contains no advertising or tracking SDKs and sells no data to third parties.
  • Exactly one optional feature makes an outbound network request, and only to an address you entered yourself. It is described under Network Use.

Information We Access

Remin may access the following on your device to provide app functionality:

  • Photos and videos, with their metadata (capture time, and location where present): travel photo curation, search and content creation
  • Your current location: recorded when you save a moment or a place from Apple Watch, and used to show where a place shortcut applies
  • Microphone audio: only while you are recording a voice memo on Apple Watch

Storage

Remin stores analysis results, settings, voice memo recordings and any notes you write locally on your device (local database and cache) for app operation.

On-Device Processing

  • Photo clustering, quality assessment, visual similarity and the search index are computed on your device using Apple's Vision and Core ML frameworks.
  • Travel stories, session names and semantic tags are generated by Apple Intelligence's on-device foundation models on supported devices. No prompt or photo is sent to a server.
  • Voice memos are recorded and kept on your device. Remin does not transcribe them — the note shown beside a memo is text you type yourself.

Network Use

Remin has no server of its own. Your photos, videos, locations, voice memos and analysis results are never transmitted anywhere.

Place shortcuts (optional). If you register a web page or image address for a place, your iPhone fetches that address over HTTPS to read the code contained in it, so that your Apple Watch can show it when you arrive. The request goes only to the host you typed in. As with any web request, that site's operator can see your IP address and the time of the request; nothing about your photos, your location or your analysis results is included. Apple Watch itself never uses the network — the iPhone passes it the finished code. If you register no address, Remin makes no outbound requests at all.

Separately, if you use iCloud Photo Library, photos may be downloaded to your device over network connections managed by iOS itself (Apple services).

Tracking

Remin does not track users. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier and no crash reporter that reports to us.

Permissions

  • Photo access: photo selection, analysis and album creation
  • Location (when in use): recording where a moment or place was saved, and matching place shortcuts to where you are
  • Microphone: recording voice memos on Apple Watch

Every permission is optional except photo access, and Remin's other features continue to work without them.

Data Deletion

When you delete the app, Remin's local data — including voice memo recordings and your notes — is removed with it. (Your original photos and albums remain yours to manage in the Photos app.)

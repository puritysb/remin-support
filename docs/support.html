Support

If you're experiencing issues with Remin, please check the items below.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • My photos don't appear: Go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Remin and enable photo access permission.
  • Analysis takes too long: Processing time varies depending on the number of photos (hundreds to thousands), iCloud download status, and device performance.
  • It seems stuck in background: Reopen the app to check progress. iOS may limit background processing depending on system conditions.
  • Where are the Apple Watch features?: Install Remin on your watch from the Watch app on iPhone. Keeping a moment, voice memos and place shortcuts all sync to the iPhone the next time both are nearby.
  • A place shortcut shows nothing: Check that the address you registered is reachable over HTTPS and that the page still contains the code. If a fetch fails, Remin keeps showing the last code it read successfully.
  • No travel story is offered: AI stories need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it enabled in iOS Settings. Everything else in Remin works without it.
  • Does Remin turn voice memos into text?: No. A memo is kept as audio you can play back, and the note beside it is yours to write. Nothing is sent anywhere to be transcribed.
  • Video generation failed or looks wrong: Long clips are trimmed to about five seconds each for pacing. If a render fails, try again with fewer photos; rendering is done on-device and needs free storage.

Contact

The easiest way to reach us is through GitHub Issues.

  • Issues: https://github.com/puritysb/remin-support/issues

What to Include in Bug Reports

  • Device model and iOS version
  • Approximate number of photos (album size)
  • Steps to reproduce (which screen, which button)
  • Screenshots if possible

Privacy

Remin analyses your photos on your device and never uploads them. The one exception to network silence is a place shortcut you register yourself, which your iPhone fetches to read its code — no photo, location or analysis data is included. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

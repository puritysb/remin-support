## About Remin

Remin turns a camera roll full of travel photos into something you can look back on — organised by itself, searchable in plain language, and ready to become a video.

### Features

- **Smart clustering** — four passes over your library: time, GPS, visual similarity, then quality
- **Journey on a map** — routes and memories laid out on an interactive map
- **Video generation** — cinematic videos built from your photos, rendered on your device
- **On this day** — browse by date and revisit the same day in past years
- **Apple Watch** — keep a moment, leave a voice memo, and get the code you need at a place you saved
- **On-device processing** — analysis and AI run on your device; your photos are never uploaded

### Requirements

- iOS 26.0 or later (iPhone and iPad), and on Mac via Designed for iPad
- watchOS 26.0 or later for the Apple Watch app

## Issues

Bug reports and feature requests are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/puritysb/remin-support/issues).

The most useful reports include your device model and OS version, roughly how many photos were involved, and the steps that led to the problem.

## About this repository

This repository only hosts the published website — the pages are generated in the app project and synced here, so direct edits to `docs/` are overwritten on the next sync. Please open an issue instead of a pull request against those files.
